Tower Tide: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Tower Tide is a single-player game for Android made by Phillip Cole ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Tower Tide does not collect, send to a server, or share any personal or device information. The game has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases, and it does not use the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves your best height, the look you picked, the looks you unlocked, and your sound and vibration settings on your own device. This never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions

The game uses the vibration permission for haptic feedback. You can turn vibration off in the game's Settings.

Children

Tower Tide is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If a future version adds advertising or any other data collection, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: philquake@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/philquake/philquake.github.io/issues.
